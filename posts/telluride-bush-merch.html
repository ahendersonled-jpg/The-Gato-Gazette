Funding a CDL League Team With Merch Sales??

The rag tag crew that is somehow running a pretty competitive CDL team is making up for lost funding by... MERCH?

Uncle Aaron · August 21, 2026 · 3 min read

With funding virtually nonexistent, the rag tag team running Telluride Bush has turned to... drumroll please... merch sales. The drive on these guys is CRAZY respectable. Talk about all eggs in one basket. They are betting their careers on this thing.

After coming out with a bang, the Telluride Bush CDL team fell flat with a disappointing finish. On top of that, they partied hard, wrecked cars in other countries, ate an astronomical amount of snacks that I ate when I was 13 playing Halo 3 at 2am, and stayed in houses WAYYYY beyond their means. What is the outcome? El Pres (owner of Barstool) has shut the wallet, and Dan "Big Cat" Katz (the Barstool Chicago head honcho) has relabeled Jersey Jerry (leader of the rag tag crew behind TBush Gaming) as a scammer. This has set a fire under the rag tag crew at TBush (Jersey Jerry, Lil Sas, and Mook). They refuse to give up. And here's the thing... I'm kind of here for it. League gaming has always revolved around big $$, but not creativity. Barstool has both big $ and creativity. This could be exactly what league gaming needs. Let's be real, all gamers have dreamt of a day where CDL is a regular on ESPN or Fox. It would be sick to see the product of our generation (gaming) finally crest the hill. Enter Barstool Sports, the little engine (turned big engine) that could. Saudis and billionaires have $, streamers have the eyes and ears, but Barstool has the $, the eyes, and the ears. We need their engine to push us (gamers) over the hill.

Question: is merch enough to fund an entire CDL team? I doubt it. Though I will say the merch is FIRE. Barstool are masters of merch and it does help spread the name, but us gamers are notorious for having deep pockets. I believe the hope is to drum up enough business to get another season under their belts and captivate the attention of El Pres. Here's the thing, remember what I said earlier? We kind of need this to happen so that we can get over that hill. I don't know how successful this merch thing will be, but I am here for it either way. I am excited to see where this could take gaming.

Will the rag tag team over at TBush Gaming fall in Valhalla, or will they be escorted to Asgard by the Valkyries? Stay tuned...
