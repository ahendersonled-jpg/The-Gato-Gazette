The Bush's Wild Week: Swept, Melting Down, and Maybe Getting Bought Out

It took Telluride Bush exactly 72 hours to turn a scoreboard problem into an ownership problem. We're not saying we called it. We're saying nobody's surprised.

THE GATO GAZETTE STAFF · Aug 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Let's set the scene. Telluride Bush — yes, Barstool's Call of Duty Challengers team, the ones with the unshaven flag and a genuine shot at relevance — rolled into Challengers Finals in Las Vegas as one of the hottest teams in the field. First place at Elite Playoffs 4. First place at Major 3 in Atlanta. Real momentum. The kind of momentum that makes you start planning a parade.

Instead, Winners Round 1 happened. OMiT, the defending champs, swept them 0-3. Straight into the Losers Bracket. The Bush clawed all the way back from that hole, which is legitimately impressive, but it wasn't enough — three teams punched their tickets to the Esports World Cup out of that Finals field, and the Bush weren't one of them.

A rough finish on its own is just a rough finish. What happened next is why we're writing this post.

Enter: The Meltdown

Jersey Jerry — one of the Barstool personalities who bankrolled this whole operation — posted a reaction to the loss that did not sound like a guy who was going to let this rock cool off quietly. He called the performance

"totally unacceptable"
and made it clear the team had shown zero fight on the biggest stage of the year.

Then the weekend got weird. Instead of a normal offseason cooldown, the team's account started posting unhinged memes under the #Unshaven hashtag. Barstool's Big Cat showed up publicly confused about what he was watching, admitting he didn't even realize the chaos was unfolding live — and half-joked that a team airing out its issues on stream might make for good content anyway.

Then Monday happened. On Wake Up Barstool, the conversation reportedly turned toward ownership questions — the kind of talk that has an entire Discord speculating about a buyout before anyone's actually confirmed anything.

What We're Watching Next

Here's the thing about the Bush: this org has never once had a boring offseason. Car crashes, ticket disputes with the league office, a Challengers Cup 1 championship banner, roster churn every few months — chaos is basically the brand at this point. Whether that's "great television" or "a sign something needs to change" probably depends on whether you're watching as a fan or signing the checks.

We'll have more the second anything official drops. Until then: stay unshaven, stay tuned.
